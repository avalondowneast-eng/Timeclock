Overtime = over 8 h/day (1.5×; over 10 h = 2×, December 1.5×). Weekend 1.5×. Holidays 2.5× (New Year’s Day, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas). Holidays 2× (MLK Day, Presidents’ Day, Juneteenth, Columbus Day, Veterans Day). Night 6pm–6am adds the night differential per hour (applies to all records). When rules overlap, the highest multiplier applies (not stacked). Pay rate in effect at each minute is used; Hourly Pay Rate shows the rate for that shift (a range if the rate changed mid-shift). Night Shift Differential is shown only on shifts that include hours between 6 pm and 6 am.
